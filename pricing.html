How this site is funded

The whole library on this site is free and it is going to stay free. That is a decision rather than a phase, and it is worth explaining what pays for it, because a free site with no visible funding is a site with an invisible business model, and invisible business models are rarely in the reader's interest.

There are five sources of revenue here, in roughly descending order of size:

  1. Display advertising. We show ads from third-party ad networks, including Google. Slots are labeled Advertisement, sit below the main content of a page, never touch navigation or buttons, and never appear on legal pages or the error page. Advertising cookies are not set before you accept them in the consent banner.
  2. Optional membership. Described in full below. It removes ads for you and adds downloadable files. It does not unlock any writing.
  3. Affiliate commissions. When we link to a mat, block, strap or bolster, some links pay a commission if you buy. Every one carries a disclosure directly beside it, not only in the footer. See the disclosure page.
  4. Paid digital products. Downloadable beginner programs and sequence bundles, sold once, in US dollars, with no subscription attached.
  5. Sponsored articles and directory placements. Sponsored articles carry the word Sponsored above the headline and the sponsor's name. A basic listing in the teacher directory is free; a featured placement is paid and labeled.

What we do not do: sell your email address, sell your personal information, put the pose library behind a paywall, use fake countdown timers or fake scarcity, or run interstitial ads that block content when you arrive.

In one line

The library is free and ad-supported. Membership is a way to pay for it directly instead, in exchange for no ads and some files. Both are legitimate ways to use this site.

Billing, in plain terms

Currency. All prices are in US dollars. Where your state charges sales tax on digital goods, it is calculated and shown at checkout before you confirm anything.

Renewal. Memberships renew automatically until you cancel. Annual memberships get a reminder email fourteen days before renewal. Monthly memberships do not, because a monthly charge is its own reminder.

Cancelling. One click in your account. There is no retention flow, no "are you sure", and nobody will email you to ask why. Access continues until the end of the period you have paid for.

Refunds. Full refund within 30 days of any membership charge, and within 30 days of a digital product purchase, for any reason or none. Email hello@stillpoint.studio and we will process it.

Price changes. We will never change the price during a term you have already paid for. If prices change, existing members are told at least 30 days before their next renewal.

What membership does not do

It does not unlock any writing. There is no members-only pose entry, no members-only sequence and no members-only guide, and there is not going to be. Everything editorial on this site is free to everyone, and paying does not buy you better information.

It does not remove third-party ads from other sites, only from this one, and only while you are signed in.

It does not include personal instruction, feedback on your practice, or anything that would amount to individual advice. We are a reference library, not a teacher. For instruction, work with a qualified teacher in a room with you; the directory is a starting point.

It does not make this site a medical or clinical resource. We publish general movement education and make no medical, therapeutic or health claims at any price. Read the health and safety disclaimer.
