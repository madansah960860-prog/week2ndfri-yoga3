The directory

A website cannot see how you move.

The one thing this site structurally cannot provide is somebody in the room watching you. This directory exists to help you find that person.

Photograph via Unsplash

Why we run a directory at all

Everything on this site is written to be read and then moved to. That works well for learning what a shape is, what it asks of you, and what usually goes wrong. It does not work at all for the thing a teacher actually does, which is watch you move and tell you what they see. No amount of written detail substitutes for that, and we would rather say so plainly than pretend otherwise.

So this directory is here to point you at people who can do the part we cannot. It is not a booking platform, we take no cut of any class fee, and we do not receive a commission when you attend.

How to choose a teacher

  • Ask what a beginner class actually involves. How long, how many people, how much instruction per posture, and whether props are provided. The answers tell you more than the style name.
  • Ask about their training. Any teacher should be able to tell you where they trained, for how many hours, and whether they hold a current registration. Ask directly; nobody reasonable minds being asked.
  • Tell them what is going on with you. Any injury, any condition, any recent surgery, whether you are pregnant. A good teacher will adapt. A teacher who waves this away is telling you something useful.
  • Ask about hands-on adjustment. Whether the teacher touches students, and whether consent is asked for each time. You are entitled to say no and it should be easy to say no.
  • Go twice before deciding. A first class in an unfamiliar room is not a fair test of anything.

What we verify, and what we do not

Listings in this directory are submitted by the studio or teacher. We check that the business exists, that the contact details work and that the listing is not making medical or therapeutic claims. We do not verify credentials, qualifications, insurance or registration status. That is your job and it is a reasonable thing to ask about before you book.

Every listing on this page today is a placeholder used to demonstrate the format. None of the businesses shown are real, none of the details have been verified, and all of them are marked as placeholders in the page source.

Before you go

Read styles explained so the schedule makes sense, and Start Here if this will be your first class.

Speak with a qualified healthcare professional before beginning any new physical practice, particularly if you are pregnant, recently injured, post-surgical or managing a health condition.

Getting listed, free

A basic directory listing costs nothing and always will. It includes your studio or teaching name, city and state, the styles you teach, one contact method, a link to your own site, and up to fifty words of description.

To be listed we ask for four things:

  • A working phone number or email address that a member of the public can reach you on.
  • A physical location, or a clear statement that you teach online only.
  • Where you trained and how many hours, which we publish as you give it and do not independently verify.
  • A description that makes no medical, therapeutic or health claims and no weight-loss claims. We will ask you to reword anything that does.

Send those to directory@stillpoint.studio or use the contact form. Listings usually go live within five business days.

Featured placement, paid

A featured placement is a paid tier. Featured listings appear above free listings in their city, carry a short expanded description of up to 150 words, and include a link to a specific class or course page rather than just a homepage.

Featured listings are labeled Featured everywhere they appear, in the same size type as the rest of the listing. Paying does not change the rules: featured listings are held to exactly the same standards on claims, and we will remove one that breaks them without a refund.
