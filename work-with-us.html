What we will and will not run

This site exists because people trust that what they read here is what we actually think. That trust is the only asset we have, and it is worth considerably more than any single campaign. So the standards below apply to every advertiser, every sponsor and every directory listing, at every budget, without exception.

We will not accept

  • Any medical, therapeutic or health claim. No product, class or service advertised here may be described as treating, curing, healing, relieving or preventing any condition. This includes back pain, anxiety, sleep, digestion, blood pressure, fertility and immunity.
  • Any weight-loss, fat-burning, slimming, detox, cleanse or fasting claim. No calorie figures, no before-and-after imagery, no body-shape commentary.
  • Supplements, diet products, or anything ingestible marketed for a health outcome.
  • Unsubstantiated superlatives. No "best", no "number one", no "guaranteed", unless you can substantiate it and are willing to show us how.
  • Fake urgency or scarcity. No countdown timers, no invented stock counters, no "only two spots left" unless it is literally true and verifiable.
  • Credential claims we cannot check. If a listing states a qualification, we publish it as your statement and we say that we have not verified it.
  • Interstitials, pop-unders, auto-playing audio, or anything that redirects a reader.

We will accept

  • Display advertising in the labeled slots described below.
  • Sponsored articles, with the word Sponsored and the sponsor's name shown above the headline.
  • Featured placements in the teacher directory, labeled as featured everywhere they appear.
  • Product review requests, on the understanding that a review is editorial and may be unfavorable, and that no payment changes that.
The standards apply at every budget. Nothing we can charge for is worth more than a reader's assumption that we mean what we write. Work with us

How sponsorship is labeled

A sponsored article carries a labeled block at the very top of the page, above the headline, stating that it is sponsored and naming the sponsor. That label is in normal-size type, not fine print, and it is present in the page for every reader on every device. The sponsor is shown the piece before publication and may correct factual errors about their own product. They may not change our conclusions, and they do not receive approval rights over anything else on the site.

How a booking works

Email advertising@stillpoint.studio with what you want to run and roughly when. We reply within two business days with availability, verified audience figures and a written quote.

Creative is reviewed against the standards on this page before anything goes live. If something needs rewording we will tell you exactly what and why.

Writing for us

We commission occasional guest guides from teachers and writers. We pay, we do not accept unpaid contributions, and we do not accept pieces written to place a link.

Pitch a specific idea in three sentences, with a note on your background, to hello@stillpoint.studio. Any credential you state will be published as your statement and marked as unverified unless you can evidence it.

Sending products for review

We accept mats, blocks, straps, bolsters and blankets for review. We keep them or donate them, we do not return them, and receiving a product creates no obligation to publish anything.

Where a review contains an affiliate link, that is disclosed beside the link. Where a product was supplied free, that is disclosed at the top of the review. See the disclosure page.
