What we collect

Information you give us directly

  • Newsletter signup. Your email address, and your first name if you choose to give it. Nothing else.
  • Contact form. Your name, email address, chosen topic, message, and your phone number if you choose to give it.
  • Membership. Your email address, your account password in hashed form, your membership status, and your saved collections. Card details are handled entirely by our payment processor and never reach our servers.
  • Directory submission. The business or teaching name, location, styles, contact details and description you send us for publication.

Information collected automatically

  • Server logs. Your IP address, the pages you request, the date and time, your browser type and version, your operating system, and the referring page if there is one. These are collected for every visitor as an ordinary part of serving a website, and are used for security and for diagnosing faults.
  • Cookies and similar technologies. Only strictly necessary storage is used before you make a choice in the consent banner. Analytics and advertising cookies are set only if you accept them. The full list is in the cookie policy.

What we do not collect

We do not collect health information, and we ask you not to send us any. We do not ask about injuries, conditions, medications, pregnancy or symptoms, and the contact form is not a place to describe them. We do not collect precise geolocation. We do not buy personal information from data brokers.

How we use it

  • To send the weekly newsletter, if you have asked for it.
  • To answer your message when you contact us.
  • To provide and bill a membership, if you have one, and to send service messages about it such as a renewal reminder or a receipt.
  • To publish a directory listing you have submitted for publication.
  • To keep the site secure, detect abuse, and diagnose faults.
  • With your consent, to measure readership in aggregate so we know which guides are actually read.
  • With your consent, to serve and measure advertising, which is what pays for the free library.

We do not use your information to make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects. We do not profile you for any purpose other than advertising, and only where you have consented to advertising cookies.

Cookies, tracking and your choices

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device by a website. We also use similar technologies such as local storage. The full inventory, with names, purposes and lifetimes, is in the cookie policy.

No non-essential cookie is set before you consent. On your first visit you will see a banner offering Accept all, Reject optional and Manage preferences. Until you choose, only strictly necessary storage is used, which is limited to remembering that the banner has been shown and recording your eventual choice.

You can change your mind at any time. Select Cookie Preferences in the footer of any page to reopen the banner and make a different choice. Rejecting optional cookies does not restrict any content on this site.

Categories

  • Strictly necessary. Page navigation, security, and remembering your consent choice. These cannot be switched off because the site does not work without them.
  • Analytics. Aggregate measurement of which pages are read. Off unless you accept.
  • Advertising. Third-party cookies used to serve and measure ads. Off unless you accept.

Browser controls

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their settings. Blocking all cookies may prevent parts of this or any other site from working. Instructions differ by browser and are in each browser's own help pages.

This site honors the Global Privacy Control signal. If your browser or extension sends a GPC signal, we treat it as a valid request to opt out of the sharing of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Third-party vendors, including Google

We use a small number of third-party service providers. Each one receives only what it needs to do its job.

  • Hosting and content delivery. Serves the pages and processes server logs, including your IP address.
  • Email service provider. Stores newsletter subscriber addresses and sends the weekly letter.
  • Payment processor. Handles membership and product payments. Card details go directly to them and never reach our servers.
  • Analytics provider. Aggregate readership measurement, only if you accept analytics cookies.
  • Advertising vendors, including Google. Serve and measure the display advertising that funds this site, only if you accept advertising cookies.
  • Font and image delivery. Typefaces are requested from Google Fonts and photographs are requested from Unsplash. Requesting a file from those services discloses your IP address to them.

Google's use of advertising cookies

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings at adssettings.google.com. You may opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info, run by the Digital Advertising Alliance, or optout.networkadvertising.org, run by the Network Advertising Initiative. Where we use any vendor not covered by those tools, you can opt out through our own consent banner by selecting Reject optional.

Google's own handling of data is described in its privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy and its advertising practices at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

How long we keep things

  • Newsletter subscribers. Until you unsubscribe, and then for 30 days in a suppression record so that we do not accidentally email you again.
  • Contact form correspondence. 24 months from the date of your last message, then deleted.
  • Membership records. For as long as the membership is active, and then for 7 years where US tax and accounting rules require us to keep transaction records.
  • Directory listings. For as long as the listing is live, plus 12 months after removal.
  • Server logs. 90 days, then deleted.
  • Consent records. 24 months, so that we can show what you chose and when.

Where a retention period has passed, information is deleted or irreversibly anonymized. We do not keep personal information indefinitely just in case it becomes useful.

Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Access to subscriber and member data is limited to the people who need it and is protected by individual accounts with multi-factor authentication. No system is perfectly secure and we do not claim otherwise. If a breach affects your personal information we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by applicable US state law.

Children's privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13 years of age and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. This is consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

You must be 13 or older to subscribe to the newsletter, to create a membership account, or to submit anything through the contact form. Each of those forms asks you to confirm this.

If you are a parent or guardian and you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, email privacy@stillpoint.studio with enough detail for us to identify the record. We will delete it promptly and confirm that we have done so.

The physical practice described on this site is general adult movement education. It is not designed or written for children, and children should practice only under the supervision of a qualified teacher and with the agreement of a parent or guardian.

California residents: CCPA and CPRA rights

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act gives you specific rights over your personal information. This section describes them and how to use them.

Categories of personal information we collect

  • Identifiers. Email address, name, IP address, and an account identifier if you are a member.
  • Commercial information. Records of memberships and digital products purchased.
  • Internet or network activity. Pages requested, referring page, browser and device type.
  • Inferences drawn for advertising. Only where you have accepted advertising cookies, and only by third-party advertising vendors rather than by us.

We collect these for the business purposes described in How we use it. We disclose them to the service providers listed in Third-party vendors.

We do not sell your personal information for money

We have never sold personal information for money and we do not intend to. However, under the CPRA the use of third-party advertising cookies can constitute sharing personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. If you accept advertising cookies, that sharing takes place. If you do not, it does not.

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

To opt out of any sharing of your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, do any one of the following:

  • Select Cookie Preferences in the footer of any page and choose Reject optional, or untick Advertising and save.
  • Send a Global Privacy Control signal from your browser or an extension. We honor it automatically.
  • Email privacy@stillpoint.studio with the subject line "Do Not Sell or Share".

We do not require an account to make this request and we do not ask for more information than we need to honor it.

Your other California rights

  • Right to know. What personal information we have collected about you, the sources, the purposes, and the categories of recipients, covering the preceding 12 months.
  • Right to delete. Deletion of the personal information we hold about you, subject to legal exceptions such as records we must keep for tax purposes.
  • Right to correct. Correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to limit use of sensitive personal information. We do not collect sensitive personal information as that term is defined, so there is nothing to limit.
  • Right to non-discrimination. We will not deny you service, charge you a different price, or give you a lower quality of service because you exercised any of these rights. The free library stays free either way.

To exercise any of these, email privacy@stillpoint.studio or write to the postal address at the foot of this page. We will acknowledge your request within 10 business days and respond within 45 calendar days, extendable once by a further 45 days if the request is complex, in which case we will tell you. We verify requests by asking you to reply from the email address concerned, or by matching details you provide against our records. An authorized agent may make a request on your behalf with written permission.

Other US states

If you live in a state with a comprehensive consumer privacy law, including Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas, Oregon or Montana, you have broadly similar rights to access, correct, delete and opt out of targeted advertising. Use the same contact route above and tell us which state you are in, and we will apply the rights available to you.

Visitors from the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom

This site is aimed at readers in the United States, but it is reachable from anywhere. If you are in the EEA, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the following applies.

Controller. Stillpoint Movement Media LLC, at the address at the foot of this page, is the controller of your personal data.

Legal bases. We rely on your consent for the newsletter and for analytics and advertising cookies; on contract for providing a membership you have paid for; and on our legitimate interests in keeping the site secure, answering your messages and diagnosing faults. Where we rely on legitimate interests we have considered your rights and concluded that they are not overridden.

Your rights. Access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and objection to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting processing carried out before withdrawal. You also have the right to complain to your national supervisory authority.

International transfers. We are based in the United States and our service providers are largely US-based, so your data is transferred to and processed in the United States. Where required, transfers are made under the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent approved mechanism.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@stillpoint.studio. We respond within one month.
