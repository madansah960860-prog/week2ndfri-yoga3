The Saturday letter

One letter a week. Nothing else.

A single pose broken down, one short sequence, and one note from the archive. Sent Saturday mornings, US Central Time.

Photograph via Unsplash

What is in it

The Saturday letter is the same site in a different format. Each email contains one pose broken down the way the entries on this site are broken down, with entry steps, cues, a suggested hold and at least two prop-supported alternatives. Then one short sequence, usually between five and twelve minutes, with hold times included. Then one note from the archive, which is generally a paragraph or two on something that came up in the week.

It goes out on Saturday mornings, US Central Time. It is one email a week and it has never been more than that. There is no drip sequence, no onboarding series, no automated follow-up asking whether you saw the last one.

It is free. There is no account to create, no free trial that becomes a charge, and nothing about the letter is gated behind the paid membership. Members get the same letter as everyone else.

What is not in it

  • No health, medical or therapeutic claims. Stillpoint publishes general movement education, and the letter follows the same rule.
  • No weight-loss framing, no calorie figures, no body commentary, and nothing about diets, cleanses or fasting.
  • No affiliate links in the body of the letter. Where a guide we link to contains one, that guide discloses it at the point of use.
  • No sponsored placements inside the letter without the word Sponsored appearing at the top of the email.

How often, and for how long

Once a week, on Saturday. If we miss a week, we miss a week; we do not send two the following Saturday to catch up. You can unsubscribe after any single email in one click and the link is in every email, at the bottom, in a normal size.

The format, every week

One pose. Names in English and Sanskrit, entry steps, cues, breath, hold, common mistakes, two prop options.

One sequence. Five to twelve minutes, with hold times and a beginner path.

One note. A short piece of writing on practice, equipment or instruction.

Read a sample first

Everything in the letter is drawn from the same material as the site. If you want to know what the writing is like, read a pose entry, a sequence or a guide before subscribing.

On email, honestly

We ask for your email address because it is the only way to reach you that does not depend on an algorithm deciding whether you should see something. That is the entire reason. It is not a lead capture and it does not feed a sales sequence.

Your address is stored with our email service provider and with nobody else. We do not sell it, rent it, trade it, or hand it to advertisers. Advertisers on this site buy display space; they do not buy your details. The privacy policy sets out exactly what is stored, for how long, and how to have it deleted.

If the letter stops being useful, unsubscribe. We would genuinely rather have a small list of people who read it than a large one of people who do not.

Prefer not to give us an email address?

Everything in the letter comes from the same material as the site, and the site requires nothing from you at all. Read the pose library, the sequences and the guides without an account, an email address or a login.
