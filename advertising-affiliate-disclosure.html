Advertising and affiliate disclosure

Every way this site earns money, stated plainly, with the FTC disclosures where they belong.

Note to readers

The whole thing in one paragraph

Stillpoint is free to read and it is paid for by advertising, by affiliate commissions, by an optional membership, by a small number of paid digital products, by occasional clearly labeled sponsored articles, and by paid featured placements in the teacher directory. That is the complete list. Nothing else pays us, nobody else pays us, and there is no arrangement not described on this page.

This disclosure is made in line with the US Federal Trade Commission's Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising (16 CFR Part 255). It appears here in full and also, more importantly, beside every affiliate link at the point where you might click it.

Display advertising

We show display advertising from third-party advertising networks, including Google. Here is how we handle it.

  • Every slot carries the word Advertisement above it, in a legible size, in the same place every time.
  • Slots sit below the main content of a page. None sits above the primary content of the fold, and none is placed so that it could be mistaken for the article.
  • No slot touches navigation, buttons, links or form controls, so a mis-tap cannot become an ad click.
  • Slots reserve their own height in the layout, so nothing on the page moves after an ad loads.
  • We cap display slots at two per page. Content density always exceeds ad density.
  • There are no advertising slots on any legal page or on the error page, including this one, and there never will be.
  • No interstitials, no pop-ups or pop-unders, no auto-playing audio, no redirects, and nothing that blocks content on arrival.
  • Advertising cookies are not set until you accept them in the consent banner. See the cookie policy.

We do not control which specific advertisements a network serves. If you see one here that makes a medical, therapeutic or weight-loss claim, tell us at advertising@stillpoint.studio and we will block the advertiser.

Affiliate links

Some links on this site to mats, blocks, straps, bolsters and blankets are affiliate links. If you follow one and buy something, we may receive a commission from the retailer. The price you pay is exactly the same either way.

Where the disclosure appears

Beside the link, above or immediately before it, in the same size text as the surrounding copy, so that you see it before you decide whether to click. It is not in a footer, not in a collapsed panel, not in grey small print, and not at the end of a long article after the links.

The standard wording we use is: The retailer links in this section are affiliate links. If you buy through one we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. It does not change what we recommend.

What a commission does not buy

  • It does not change what we recommend. The recommendation is written first and the link is added afterwards.
  • We do not accept payment in exchange for a positive review, a ranking, or a mention.
  • No brand sees a review before it goes live, and no brand has approval rights over any editorial content.
  • Where a product was supplied to us free of charge, that is stated at the top of the review, separately from any affiliate disclosure.
  • Products we do not think are worth buying are not linked at all, whatever the commission.

Programs we participate in

We will list every affiliate program we participate in here by name, along with the date we joined it, once those relationships are confirmed. Until then, treat every retailer link on this site as though it may pay us a commission, because the disclosure beside each one says so.

Where you would rather not use an affiliate link, the alternative is straightforward: note the product, open a new tab, and search for it directly. Nothing on this site is withheld from you for doing that, and we would rather you buy the right thing than the linked thing.

Sponsored content and brand partnerships

Occasionally a brand pays us to publish an article. When that happens:

  • The word Sponsored appears at the very top of the page, above the headline, in normal-size type, together with the name of the sponsor.
  • The label is present for every reader on every device, and it is not removed when the article is shared.
  • The sponsor may review the piece before publication only to correct factual errors about their own product. They may not change our conclusions.
  • A sponsorship buys one page. It does not buy a mention in a pose entry, a sequence, a guide, the glossary or the directory.
  • Sponsored articles are held to exactly the same claims standards as everything else: no medical, therapeutic, health or weight-loss claims, at any price.

We turn down more sponsorship than we accept, and the usual reason is a claim we cannot let stand. The standards appear on the work with us page.

Membership, digital products and directory placements

Membership

An optional membership costs $6.00 per month or $54.00 per year in US dollars. It removes advertising for the member, and adds downloadable sequence PDFs, printable pose cards and saved collections. It unlocks no writing. Every pose entry, every sequence and every guide is free to everyone, member or not, and that is not going to change. See pricing.

Digital products

We sell four downloadable products, bought once, in US dollars, with no subscription attached. They are compilations and expansions of material on the site, formatted for printing. All four are included with a membership. Full details and prices are on the pricing page.

Teacher directory

A basic listing in the teacher directory is free and always will be. A featured placement is paid, appears above free listings in its city, and is labeled Featured everywhere it appears, in the same size type as the rest of the listing.

Paying for a featured placement does not change the standards. A featured listing that makes a medical, therapeutic or weight-loss claim is removed without a refund. We do not verify credentials for any listing, paid or free, and we say so on the directory page.

Editorial independence, and how to complain

The editorial rule here is simple and it is not negotiable: what we publish is what we actually think, and no commercial relationship changes it.

  • Recommendations are written before any link is added.
  • Advertisers have no visibility of editorial content before publication.
  • No advertiser, sponsor or affiliate partner has ever asked us to remove or change a piece of editorial content, and if one did, we would say so on this page.
  • We publish corrections when we are wrong, with a dated note, and we do not quietly rewrite pieces once they're live.

If you think we have got this wrong

If you find a link on this site that you believe is an undisclosed affiliate link, a sponsored article that is not labeled, or an advertisement that makes a health or weight-loss claim, tell us. We treat that as a serious error and we will fix it the same day where we can.
