Sequence · 30 min

Thirty minutes, unhurried on purpose.

Twenty-two positions covering standing, seated, prone and supine work, with a chair or wall variation listed for every single line in the order.

Go to the timer

Photograph via Unsplash

About this practice

This is the long one, and it is the sequence to use on the day you have room for it rather than the one to aspire to daily. Thirty minutes covers standing work, balance, seated work, one prone position, floor work and a full three-minute rest at the end.

The pace is deliberately unhurried, which is not the same as easy. Holds run between sixty and ninety seconds for the standing positions, which is long enough that the legs have opinions about it. If you have been practicing the 10-Minute Morning for a few weeks, this will feel like a step up in duration rather than in difficulty.

Unhurried is not the same as easy. Thirty minutes at this pace asks more than ten minutes at any pace. Gentle Full Practice

Every line in the order carries a beginner path, and the full path is written out below. Taking every beginner option gives you a complete thirty-minute practice that uses a wall and a chair throughout and asks nothing of your floor-to-standing transitions.

Rest whenever you want to. There are two scheduled rests in the order and you are welcome to add more. Coming out of a shape early is a decision, not a failure, and the timer will keep going without you.

The essentials

Total duration. 30 min

Difficulty. Beginner to intermediate

Positions. 22

Props required. A mat, two blocks and a strap. A wall within reach.

Space needed. About 7 feet by 4 feet.

Best time to use it. The day you have the most room in your calendar.

The order, position by position

Total running time 30 min. Every line carries its hold time and a beginner path.

  1. 2 min Child's Pose Bālāsana Knees wide, forehead resting on the floor or a block. Two full minutes. Let the breath settle before anything else happens. Beginner path: Blanket behind the knees and a cushion under the forehead.
  2. 1 min 30 sec Cat and Cow Marjaryāsana and Bitilāsana Twelve unhurried rounds. Exhale to round, inhale to open. Let the head follow the spine rather than leading it. Beginner path: Do it seated in a chair with the hands on the knees.
  3. 1 min Thread the Needle, right Parśva Bālāsana Right arm slides under the body, right shoulder and the side of the head rest down. Hips stay high over the knees. Beginner path: Rotate half as far and put a cushion under the head.
  4. 1 min Thread the Needle, left Parśva Bālāsana Second side. Expect the two sides to feel different from each other. Beginner path: Rotate half as far and put a cushion under the head.
  5. 1 min Downward-Facing Dog Adho Mukha Śvānāsana Knees generously bent, spine long, pressing the floor away through the hands. Heels reaching down but not required to land. Beginner path: Palms flat on a wall at hip height, feet walked back.
  6. 1 min Standing Forward Fold Uttānāsana Walk the feet up to the hands. Knees soft, head hanging, hands on the floor or on two blocks. Beginner path: Two blocks at their tallest setting under the hands, knees well bent.
  7. 1 min Mountain Pose Tāḍāsana Roll up slowly. Weight even across both feet, ribs settled, six even breaths standing still. Beginner path: Stand with your back lightly against a wall.
  8. 1 min 30 sec Warrior II, right foot forward Vīrabhadrāsana II Feet three to four feet apart, right knee bent over the ankle and tracking toward the little toe, arms level. Beginner path: Stance two and a half feet, knee bent halfway, hands on hips.
  9. 1 min 30 sec Warrior II, left foot forward Vīrabhadrāsana II Second side. Press the outer edge of the back foot down and keep the torso upright in the middle of the stance. Beginner path: Stance two and a half feet, knee bent halfway, hands on hips.
  10. 1 min 15 sec Triangle Pose, right Utthita Trikoṇāsana Straight legs, torso tipping sideways over the front leg, lower hand on a block at its tallest height. Beginner path: Block at its tallest height, and rest the top hand on the hip.
  11. 1 min 15 sec Triangle Pose, left Utthita Trikoṇāsana Second side. Length along the underside of the torso matters far more than how low the hand goes. Beginner path: Block at its tallest height, and rest the top hand on the hip.
  12. 1 min Tree Pose, standing on the left leg Vṛkṣāsana Right foot to the inner calf or thigh, never against the side of the knee. Eyes on one still point. Beginner path: Right toes on the floor, left fingertips on a wall.
  13. 1 min Tree Pose, standing on the right leg Vṛkṣāsana Second side. Step out and back in as many times as you need to. Beginner path: Left toes on the floor, right fingertips on a wall.
  14. 1 min 30 sec Child's Pose, first rest Bālāsana Down to the floor. Ninety seconds of doing nothing before the second half of the practice. Beginner path: Blanket behind the knees, bolster under the chest.
  15. 1 min 30 sec Low Lunge, right foot forward Añjaneyāsana Right foot forward, left knee down on a folded blanket, hips settling forward. Hands on blocks either side of the foot. Beginner path: Blocks at their tallest height, and stay upright rather than reaching forward.
  16. 1 min 30 sec Low Lunge, left foot forward Añjaneyāsana Second side. This is usually the longest ninety seconds in the sequence. Beginner path: Blocks at their tallest height, and stay upright rather than reaching forward.
  17. 2 min Bridge Pose Setu Bandha Sarvāṅgāsana On the back, feet hip-width and parallel, close to the hips. Lift and lower six times on the breath, then hold. Beginner path: Lift two inches only, or rest on a block under the pelvis.
  18. 1 min 15 sec Supine twist, knees to the right Supta Matsyendrāsana Knees drawn in and lowered to the right onto a blanket. Left shoulder heavy on the floor. Beginner path: Blanket or bolster under the knees so they rest on something.
  19. 1 min 15 sec Supine twist, knees to the left Supta Matsyendrāsana Second side, same setup. Keep the head facing up if turning it is uncomfortable. Beginner path: Blanket or bolster under the knees so they rest on something.
  20. 1 min Bound Angle Pose Baddha Koṇāsana Sitting up, soles of the feet together, knees out to the sides. Sit tall rather than pressing the knees down. Beginner path: Sit on a folded blanket with a block under each knee.
  21. 1 min Seated Forward Fold Paścimottānāsana Legs straight out in front, folding from the hips with the spine long. Strap around the feet if the hands do not reach. Beginner path: Blanket under the hips, knees bent, strap around the feet.
  22. 3 min Corpse Pose Śavāsana Flat on the back, arms a little away from the sides, legs relaxed outward. Three full minutes. Let the timer end it. Beginner path: Blanket roll under the knees and a second blanket over you.

The beginner path, in full

Every line in the order above carries a short beginner note. Here is the same path written out as one continuous set of choices, so you can read it before you begin rather than while the timer is running.

  • Take every kneeling position with a folded blanket behind the knees, and use Child's Pose with a bolster whenever you want a longer rest.
  • Take Downward-Facing Dog at the wall with the palms flat at hip height throughout, all three times it appears.
  • Use two blocks at their tallest height under the hands in every forward fold and in Triangle.
  • Take Warrior II with the feet two and a half feet apart and the front knee bent only halfway, hands on the hips rather than out at shoulder height.
  • Take Tree Pose with the toes of the lifted foot on the floor and fingertips on a wall.
  • In Bridge Pose, lift only two or three inches, or slide a block under the pelvis and rest on it.
  • In the seated forward fold, sit up on a folded blanket, bend the knees, and loop the strap around the feet rather than reaching for them.
  • In the final rest, roll a blanket under the knees and put a second blanket over you.

If you own no props at all

Two hardback books stand in for two blocks and a bathrobe belt stands in for a strap. A folded bath towel works as a blanket. The wall matters more than any of the props here, because it is what makes the standing balance and the shoulder work available without load.

How to shorten it

For a twenty-minute version, drop Triangle on both sides, the two Low Lunges and the seated forward fold. For a fifteen-minute version, keep the opening four positions, both Warrior IIs, Bridge, both twists and the final rest.
