The guides

Longer reading, on everything but the poses.

The parts of a practice that have nothing to do with any single posture: habit, instruction, equipment and the honest limits of learning from a page.

Photograph via Unsplash

What sits here

The pose library tells you what a shape is. The sequences tell you what order to put shapes in. These guides deal with everything else, which turns out to be most of what determines whether a practice actually happens: how a habit forms and why it breaks, what an instruction actually means when a teacher says it, and what equipment does and does not change.

Each one is a long read rather than a listicle, and each one is free with no account required. They are supported by the same display advertising and affiliate arrangements described on the disclosure page. Where a guide contains an affiliate link, it says so at the point of the link, not only in the footer.

Also worth reading

Start Here is the beginner's guide and the place to begin if you have never practiced.

Styles explained covers what happens in a Hatha, Vinyasa, Yin, Restorative, Iyengar or Ashtanga class.

Practice at home covers the physical setup: space, floor, light and storage.
