Sequence · 8 min

Eight minutes, without leaving the room.

Eleven seated and standing positions built around a chair and a wall, for shoulders and hips that have been folded into the same angle since morning.

Go to the timer

Photograph via Unsplash

About this practice

This sequence exists because the practice most people actually need during a working day is the one they will do in the clothes they are already wearing, in the room they are already in, without getting on the floor. Every position here is done either sitting on a chair, standing behind it, or with hands on a wall.

A chair is a genuinely useful prop rather than a substitute for one. It raises the floor to a convenient height, it gives a balance point, and it lets the hips work in a supported position. Nothing in this sequence is a reduced version of a floor practice; these are the chair versions, and they are complete.

No mat, no floor, no change of clothes. That was the entire design brief. Desk Reset

Use a chair with four legs on the floor. A wheeled office chair will move underneath you and is not safe for the standing positions. If the only chair available is on wheels, push it against a wall so it cannot roll, and skip the two standing positions that use it.

The whole thing takes eight minutes and none of it requires anyone else to leave the room.

The essentials

Total duration. 8 min

Difficulty. All levels

Positions. 11

Props required. A sturdy chair and a clear stretch of wall.

Space needed. A chair and about 4 feet of wall.

Best time to use it. Between meetings, in office clothes, without a mat.

The order, position by position

Total running time 8 min. Every line carries its hold time and a beginner path.

  1. 45 sec Seated Mountain Pose Tāḍāsana, chair Sit toward the front of the chair, both feet flat and hip-width, spine tall from the sitting bones, shoulders released. Beginner path: Books under the feet if they do not reach the floor comfortably.
  2. 1 min Seated Cat and Cow Marjaryāsana and Bitilāsana, chair Hands on the knees. Exhale and round the back, inhale and lift the chest. Eight slow rounds, small movements. Beginner path: Keep the range very small and let the head follow the spine.
  3. 30 sec Seated side bend, right Pārśva Sukhāsana, chair Left hand on the chair seat, right arm reaching up and over to the left. Let the right sitting bone stay heavy. Beginner path: Reach only to shoulder height and keep the elbow bent.
  4. 30 sec Seated side bend, left Pārśva Sukhāsana, chair Second side. The two sides will not feel the same and there is nothing to correct about that. Beginner path: Reach only to shoulder height and keep the elbow bent.
  5. 45 sec Seated twist, right Ardha Matsyendrāsana, chair Both feet flat. Lengthen up on the inhale, rotate to the right on the exhale, hands resting lightly on the chair. Beginner path: Hold the chair loosely. Do not lever yourself around with the arms.
  6. 45 sec Seated twist, left Ardha Matsyendrāsana, chair Second side, same instruction. Keep both sitting bones on the seat throughout. Beginner path: Hold the chair loosely and rotate half as far.
  7. 45 sec Wall chest opener, both sides Bhitti Vakṣaḥ Vistāra Stand side-on to a wall, palm flat against it at shoulder height, and turn the chest slowly away. Twenty seconds each side. Beginner path: Start with the hand at hip height and turn only a few degrees.
  8. 1 min Standing half forward bend at the wall Ardha Uttānāsana Palms flat on the wall at hip height, feet walked back until the arms are straight and the torso is horizontal. Beginner path: Step closer to the wall and bend the knees generously.
  9. 45 sec Standing figure-four at the chair, right Eka Pāda Utkaṭāsana Hold the chair back with both hands. Cross the right ankle over the left thigh and sit back a few inches. Beginner path: Keep the standing leg almost straight and sit back only an inch or two.
  10. 45 sec Standing figure-four at the chair, left Eka Pāda Utkaṭāsana Second side. Keep the hips level rather than dropping into one side. Beginner path: Keep the standing leg almost straight and sit back only an inch or two.
  11. 30 sec Seated close, even breath Tāḍāsana, chair Back on the chair, feet flat, hands resting on the thighs. Six even breaths, and then back to work. Beginner path: Nothing to change here. Just sit and count six breaths.

The beginner path, in full

Every line in the order above carries a short beginner note. Here is the same path written out as one continuous set of choices, so you can read it before you begin rather than while the timer is running.

  • Sit toward the front of the chair with both feet flat on the floor and your knees over your ankles. If your feet do not reach the floor, put a book or two underneath them.
  • In the seated Cat and Cow, keep the movement small. This is not about how far the spine moves, it is about moving it at all.
  • In the side bends, let the opposite sitting bone stay heavy on the chair rather than lifting off it.
  • In the seated twist, hold the side of the chair lightly rather than pulling yourself further around with it. The rotation comes from the middle of the back.
  • For the wall chest opener, start with the hand low, at hip height, and raise it only if the shoulder is comfortable.
  • In the standing half forward bend at the wall, bend the knees as much as you like and step closer to the wall to make it easier.
  • For the standing figure-four, hold the back of the chair with both hands and keep the standing knee softly bent.
  • If standing is not available today, do the first six positions seated and finish with the closing breath. That is a complete practice.

If you own no props at all

A dining chair and any clear wall are the only requirements, and almost every workplace and home has both. If there is no wall available, a closed door works for the chest opener and a desk edge works for the half forward bend as long as the desk cannot slide.

How to shorten it

The first six positions take four minutes and are all seated. That is the version to use when there are only a few minutes between calls. If you have two minutes, do the seated Cat and Cow and both seated twists.
