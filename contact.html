What we cannot answer

We cannot tell you whether a particular posture is appropriate for your body, your injury or your condition. That requires someone who can assess you, and we are not able to do that from an email.

Questions of that kind belong with a qualified healthcare professional, or with a qualified teacher in a room with you. Please do not send us descriptions of symptoms or injuries; we will not be able to help and we would rather say so up front.

Accessibility problems

If any part of this site is difficult or impossible for you to use, tell us and we will fix it. Include the page, the browser or assistive technology you are using, and what happened.

We aim to acknowledge accessibility reports within two business days and to fix confirmed problems within 30 days. See the accessibility statement.

Corrections policy

If we publish something wrong, we correct it and say so. Substantive corrections carry a dated note at the foot of the entry. Typographical fixes are made silently.

We do not remove corrections once we've posted them, and we do not quietly rewrite history. Email corrections@stillpoint.studio.

Practice safely

Stillpoint publishes general movement education, not medical advice. Nothing here treats, prevents or improves any medical condition. Speak with a qualified healthcare professional before beginning a new physical practice, particularly if you are pregnant, recently injured, post-surgical or managing a health condition, and stop if you feel pain. Work with a qualified teacher for hands-on guidance, because a website cannot see how you move.
